Privacy Policy for SMS Kavach

Effective Date: October 2026

App Package: org.mango.sentinelapp

Developer Email: mangogolahe@gmail.com

🔒 100% On-Device Privacy Guarantee: SMS Kavach is an offline-first, privacy-focused security guardian. Your contacts, address book, SMS content, and WhatsApp messages are analyzed 100% locally on your device. We NEVER collect, upload, or transmit your private contacts or message text to any remote server or cloud database.

1. Information Processing & Collection

SMS Kavach is engineered with privacy by design. Here is how data is handled on your device:

  • SMS & WhatsApp Messages: Intercepted locally in real-time and evaluated by on-device AI algorithms. Message text is stored strictly on your local device storage inside encrypted databases and is never sent to external servers.
  • Phone Contact Whitelist: Numbers added to your "Friends & Family" trusted list remain strictly on your device's local encrypted storage. We never upload your contacts to any server.
  • Hardware-Backed Encryption: Local logs and saved spam messages are encrypted on-device using AES-256 GCM encryption via Android's hardware-backed KeyStore.

2. Permissions Requested & Purpose

SMS Kavach requests minimal permissions necessary to provide security features:

  • RECEIVE_SMS & READ_SMS: Core requirement to detect and alert against incoming SMS spam, phishing links, and scam messages.
  • READ_PHONE_STATE: Optional permission used solely to warn if transactional OTPs arrive during an active phone call to prevent live call extortion.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Optional permission used to display real-time Threat Warning Popup Cards over other apps when a scam is detected.
  • BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE: Optional permission used to scan incoming WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business notification previews locally for scams and phishing links.

3. Third-Party Services & Diagnostics

SMS Kavach integrates limited, trusted third-party SDKs for advertising and basic diagnostics:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display non-intrusive banner advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (advertising ID) and coarse location for ad serving in compliance with Google Play Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crash Reporting: Used to collect anonymous performance metrics and crash diagnostic logs to improve app stability. No personally identifiable information (PII) or message content is ever sent to Firebase.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over all data stored by SMS Kavach:

  • You can delete individual spam messages or wipe the entire stored list anytime using the "Delete All" button on the Home Screen.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing app storage in Android Settings completely erases all local encrypted logs and preferences permanently.

5. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. All local data is protected by hardware-backed 256-bit AES-256 GCM encryption. Model update payloads downloaded over the air are authenticated using SHA-256 hashes and RSA digital signatures to prevent tampering.

6. Children's Privacy

SMS Kavach does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
