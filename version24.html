100% On-Device Privacy Commitment: SMS Kavach runs threat detection completely locally on your device. Your SMS messages, phone status, and personal data are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any external servers.

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy explains how SMS Kavach ("we", "us", or "our") handles permissions and information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

SMS Kavach does NOT collect, store on servers, or transmit any personal data.

3. Permissions We Use & Why

SMS Kavach requests sensitive permissions strictly for its core functionality as an SMS spam blocker and phishing threat guardian:
